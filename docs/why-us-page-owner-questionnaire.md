# Why Us Page Owner Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire to gather final business input before improving the `Why Us` page.

## 1) Brand Positioning

1. In one sentence, why should a buyer choose Sahara Cars over competitors?
2. What are the top 3 unique strengths of Sahara Cars today?
3. Which customer types are most important right now? (e.g., first-time buyers, premium buyers, import buyers)
4. What brand tone do you want the page to feel like? (e.g., premium, friendly, expert, fast)

## 2) Trust and Credibility

5. What trust promises can we confidently state publicly? (verified stock, inspections, paperwork support, etc.)
6. Do we have any certifications, licenses, or memberships we should show?
7. What numbers can we publish? (cars sold, years in business, repeat customers, response time)
8. What proof can we add immediately? (customer testimonials, delivery photos, review ratings)

## 3) Process Clarity

9. What exact buying process should the page present? (step-by-step from inquiry to handover)
10. Which steps usually cause confusion for customers today?
11. What support is included after purchase?
12. What is the realistic expected response time after a new inquiry?

## 4) Inventory and Value

13. What vehicle categories should be highlighted first? (SUV, luxury, budget, family, etc.)
14. Which value points matter most to buyers? (price transparency, financing help, import options, condition quality)
15. What guarantees can we provide and stand behind?
16. Are there recurring offers or campaigns we should feature?

## 5) Social Proof Content

17. Can you provide at least 5 real customer testimonials with names/initials and locations?
18. Can we use customer photos or vehicle handover photos? (Yes/No + limits)
19. Do we have partner logos we can display publicly? (banks, insurers, logistics)
20. Are there any recent success stories worth turning into short case studies?

## 6) Conversion and CTA Decisions

21. What is the single primary CTA for this page? (WhatsApp, call, browse inventory, order request)
22. What should be the secondary CTA?
23. Which phone/WhatsApp contact should be shown for highest conversion?
24. Should we include a short lead form on the page? If yes, what fields are required?

## 7) SEO and Local Visibility

25. What target keywords should this page rank for? (English + Swahili)
26. Which cities/locations should we mention for local search relevance?
27. What exact business name, address, and contact details should be consistent on this page?
28. Are there competitor pages you like and want us to benchmark?

## 8) Visual and Content Preferences

29. Which current sections should stay unchanged?
30. Which sections should be removed or rewritten completely?
31. Do you prefer a short page (fast read) or long page (detailed explanation)?
32. Are there legal/compliance statements we must include?

## 9) Approval and Ownership

33. Who gives final approval for content and design?
34. What is your target deadline for publishing the improved page?
35. How often should this page be reviewed/updated? (monthly/quarterly)
